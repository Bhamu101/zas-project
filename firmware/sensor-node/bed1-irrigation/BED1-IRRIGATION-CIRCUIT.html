Bed 1 — Irrigation Circuit, Built in Three Stages

Three objectives, but only one change of wiring. Stage 1 gets the pump running from the app. Stage 2 adds the probe. Stage 3 adds nothing physical at all — the automatic loop is already in the firmware, waiting on a flag.

Stage 1 · now Manual irrigation Tap Start in the app, the pump runs for the requested time and stops. wire: ESP32 · K1 · R385
Stage 2 · next Moisture on the dashboard The probe reports VWC %, the dashboard shows Bed 1's real reading. wire: + soil probe
Stage 3 · last Automatic irrigation Below 32 % VWC the node waters itself, stopping at 48 %. wire: nothing — one flag

Wiring — what you build today

⚠
Two things to get right before you power it

The boot glitch. An ESP32 pin floats LOW out of reset and LOW closes an active-LOW relay — so a careless sketch pulses the pump at every boot. setup() writes GPIO18 HIGH before pinMode(); those lines are order-sensitive. If you hear a click at reset, stop and check them.

Dry running. Prime the suction hose and keep the inlet under water. Seconds of dry running wears the R385's diaphragm, and at this stage nothing in software knows whether there is water.

Bring-up stage 1

Each step adds one thing to a working state, so a fault points at the step that caused it. PROBE_FITTED and AUTO_AT_BOOT are both false in the sketch already.

  1. Pump alone on the adapter. No ESP32, no relay. Centre pin of the barrel jack is +. Confirm it runs and primes, and meter its running current — that number decides whether this adapter can carry the valve later.
  2. ESP32 alone. Flash bed1-irrigation.ino, power over USB, Serial at 115200. It should print STAGE: 1 — manual irrigation only (no probe) and Pump: OFF, then connect and show its IP.
  3. Relay, no load. Wire VCC, GND and IN1 only; leave COM/NO empty. Hit http://<ip>/irrigate?sec=10&key=… — K1 should click in, hold 10 s, release. Reset the ESP32 a few times and confirm no click at boot.
  4. Add the 12 V side. F1, C1, K1 COM and NO, pump in a bucket. Same 10 s cycle: pump runs, stops on its own.
  5. Drive it from the app. Put ESP32_BASE_URL and ESP32_SHARED_SECRET in backend/.env, restart the API, then tap Start on Bed 1. That is objective 1 done.

If it misbehaves

  • Relay clicks at boot or reset. The active-LOW glitch — digitalWrite(pin, HIGH) must run before pinMode(pin, OUTPUT).
  • Relay clicks but the pump doesn't run. Contacts or 12 V side, not the drive. Meter K1 COM and NO to ground while it is on.
  • Adapter buzzes or drops out at pump start. It is hitting its 2 A limit. Check C1's polarity and value; if it persists, the adapter is undersized for this pump.
  • Pump runs but no water. Lost prime, or the strainer is blocked.
  • App says irrigating, nothing happens. The backend cannot reach the node — check ESP32_BASE_URL, and that /status answers from the machine running the API.

Stage 2 · once stage 1 is solid

Adding the probe

Three wires and a flag. This is the only other time the wiring changes.

FromToNote
ESP32 3V3Probe VCCnot 5 V — keeps AOUT inside the ADC's range
ESP32 GNDProbe GND
ESP32 GPIO34Probe AOUTADC1 — ADC2 pins stop working once WiFi is up
  • Set PROBE_FITTED = true, reflash.
  • Probe in air → /raw, note the number. Probe in water → /raw again. Then /calibrate?dry=XXXX&wet=YYYY&key=…, and copy both into dryRaw/wetRaw when you are happy.
  • Dry must be the higher raw number on a capacitive probe. If VWC sits at 0 or 100, the two are swapped.
  • Push the probe into the bed near where water actually lands, not at the edge.
  • The dashboard picks the reading up within ~5 s. That is objective 2.

Stage 3 · once readings look right

Turning on automatic irrigation

No wiring. Set AUTO_AT_BOOT = true and reflash — or leave the flag alone and call /auto?on=1&key=… to arm it without a rebuild.

  • Below 32 % VWC the node starts a cycle on its own and stops at 48 %. Those mirror CROP_TARGETS["Covo"] in backend/main.py — change them in both places, or the dashboard and the node will disagree.
  • A 10 min cap ends a cycle if moisture never reaches 48 %, and a 15 min soak blocks re-triggering so water has time to reach the probe.
  • Auto cannot arm without a probe — /auto?on=1 returns an error at stage 1, and the trigger itself requires a plausible reading. A pulled-out probe stops watering rather than starting it.
  • Manual still wins: tapping Start pre-empts a running automatic cycle.
  • Watch the first few cycles. If it keeps hitting the 10 min cap, water is not reaching the probe.
