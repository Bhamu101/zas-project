Bed 1 — Automatic Irrigation Bench Circuit

One capacitive probe, one mini submersible pump, one ESP32. The node reads the probe, decides on its own when to water (Covo: start below 32 % VWC, stop at 48 %), and the backend polls it so the dashboard shows the real reading and the cycle it triggered.

Two ways to kill this test in seconds

The mini pump is 3–6 V — keep it off the fused 12 V bus that feeds the valve. And put the pump in the bucket before the probe goes into dry soil; the moment VWC reads under 32 % the node will switch it on, and it must not run dry.

Physical layout

  • Pump fully under water in the bucket, tubing from its outlet into the pot.
  • Probe pushed into the pot's soil up to its line, near where the tube drips — a probe 10 cm from the water lands you on the 60 s safety cap before it ever reads 48 %.
  • Probe electronics (the top of the board) stay dry.

Before you start

  1. Flash bed1-auto-irrigation-test.ino. Serial Monitor at 115200 — note the IP.
  2. In backend/.env: ESP32_BASE_URL=http://<that ip> and ESP32_SHARED_SECRET= the same key as the sketch. Restart uvicorn.
  3. Probe in the air → open http://<ip>/raw?key=…, write down raw. Probe in a cup of water → again. Then /calibrate?dry=XXXX&wet=YYYY&key=…. Copy the two numbers into dryRaw / wetRaw when you're happy.
  4. Pump into the bucket, tube into the pot, probe into the dry soil. Open the dashboard.

3 · stop Pump stops on its own Pump OFF — moisture reached optimal band (or auto safety cap at 60 s — move the tube closer) Pump OFF; state optimal if ≥ 48 %; activity Irrigation complete — Bed 1
4 · soak Nothing for 90 s even if you pull the probe out Soak period: 90 s before auto may re-trigger /status shows soakRemainingSec counting down; dashboard idle
5 · fault Unplug AOUT SENSOR FAULT, no cycle starts sensorOk: false on Bed 1; VWC holds the last good value; manual Start still works
6 · manual Tap Start on the app BED 1 IRRIGATION STARTED — MANUAL Same as stage 2 but cycleType: manual; Stop cuts it short

If it doesn't behave

  • VWC stuck at 0 or 100. Calibration is backwards or unmeasured — dry must be the higher raw number on a capacitive probe.
  • sensorOk: false with the probe plugged in. AOUT is on an ADC2 pin, or the probe is powered from 5 V and AOUT is pinned above 3.3 V. Check the pin is 34 and VCC is 3V3.
  • ESP32 reboots when the pump kicks in. USB can't supply the inrush — add C1 across +5 V/GND or power the pump from a separate 5 V supply with grounds joined.
  • Pump runs but VWC never moves. Water isn't reaching the probe — the 60 s cap will keep tripping. Move the tube or the probe.
  • Dashboard still shows 18 %. ESP32_BASE_URL not set or not reachable from where the backend runs; systemStatus.esp32Online tells you which.
